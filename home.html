About Us

Smart Parking Real-Time Tracking QR Payment AI Prediction

Parkzen is a smart parking management system designed to simplify vehicle parking operations. It allows users to register vehicles, track entry and exit in real-time, and monitor parking availability efficiently.

With QR-based payments, smart slot suggestions, and live status updates, Parkzen reduces congestion and makes parking faster, smarter, and more efficient.
